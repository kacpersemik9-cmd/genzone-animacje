# GenZone Referral Program — spec ad #2 · "The link is the product"

Status: **Idea 2 chosen (2026-10-04). PREVIEW v1 delivered: 16:9, 60 fps, picture + original score, no VO, no SFX.**

## Concept
One continuous camera move through the real Referral Dashboard. It starts in macro on your referral
link, pulls out to the whole screen, glides down to "What you earn" where the real rows lift off
in gold, returns to the link, then the frame closes into a circle where the GenZone wave mark draws itself.

## Facts used (only these)
Up to $500 per Dubai company (License + 1 Residency Visa row) · up to $400 per US LLC (Done-For-You row) ·
referral stays attached to your link · terms fixed when earned. No old rates, no totals, no income claims.

## Safety
The screenshot is cropped above the pipeline and tracking table (no empty states). The referral code is blurred in the
link field and in "Referred by". No real names are shown.

glide, G-major chord lands with the logo (18.0 s) and rings under the made-by card. Master −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP.
