## 2. Three ideas — pick one

### Idea A — "You already get asked"
**Insight:** creators and consultants already answer "How do I open a Dubai company / a US LLC?"
for free. **Turn:** answer with your link, get paid when they form.
Opens on the question as huge kinetic type, a cut to the real link field, then the gold figures.
Feels: smart, knowing, made for people with an audience.

### Idea B — "One link"
**Insight:** the whole program is one object, your link. The ad follows that link like a
camera on a wire: it gets copied, it travels, the referral moves through the real pipeline
stages, it lands as a gold figure.
Feels: clean, product-led, very "Apple keynote".

### Idea C — "Every intro has a price tag"
**Insight:** every intro you make already has a price on it, you just don't see it. Each
package name from the real "What you earn" table gets its price stamped on it in gold, beat by
beat, like a rate card printing itself.
Feels: punchy, rhythmic, the most "$"-forward of the three.

**CHOSEN: A** (client pick, 2026-10-04). **My pick was A.** It speaks straight to the four audiences, the hook works with the sound off,
and it earns the "$500" instead of opening on it. B is the safest; C is the loudest but the
most rate-card-heavy.

## 7. Music (original, made in code)

  no limiter squashing the voice), opening up in the word gaps and beat 6.
- Master: **−14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP**.
